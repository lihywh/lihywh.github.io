Amao Dash

独立的 Android AdMob 报表应用（包名 com.amao.dash）

准备中 · 尚未发布

Amao Dash 帮助 Google AdMob 发布商在手机上查看自己账号的收入与报表：连接您自己的 Google 账号后，应用通过 HTTPS 直接从 Google 获取数据，并在设备上展示。

主要功能

  • 收入概览：查看账号整体收入与关键指标。
  • 多维报表：按应用、国家/地区、广告单元等维度细分数据。
  • 16 种界面语言：包含简体中文与繁体中文。
  • 演示模式：明确标注，仅使用合成的示例数据，无需连接账号。
  • 本地处理：报表数据在设备上解析与展示；开发者不运营后端服务器，也不建立用户账号数据库。

以上描述基于当前正在准备的初始构建，最终以实际发布版本为准。

工作方式

使用您自己的 Google 账号授权，应用仅申请 AdMob 只读权限（admob.readonly），不能修改您的 AdMob 数据或设置。数据如何处理的完整说明见隐私政策。

Amao Dash

An independent Android app for Google AdMob reporting (package com.amao.dash)

In preparation · not yet released

Amao Dash helps Google AdMob publishers view their own account's earnings and reports on Android: after you connect your own Google account, the app fetches data directly from Google over HTTPS and displays it on your device.

Main features

  • Earnings overview: see account-level earnings and key metrics.
  • Multi-dimension reports: break data down by app, country/region, and ad unit.
  • 16 interface languages: including Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
  • Demo mode: clearly labelled, uses synthetic sample data only, no account needed.
  • Local processing: report data is parsed and displayed on your device; the developer operates no backend server and keeps no user account database.

The description above reflects the initial build currently in preparation; the released version is authoritative.

How it works

You authorize with your own Google account, and the app requests only the AdMob read-only scope (admob.readonly); it cannot change your AdMob data or settings. See the privacy policy for the full description of how data is handled.

Release status

The app is in preparation and has not been released; no download channel is offered. This page will be updated once a release channel is confirmed.

Note

Amao Dash is a third-party tool made by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with, partnered with, or endorsed by Google.
