纸上猜飞机隐私说明

适用 Android 包名 com.amao.paperplanes，生效日期 2026 年 11 月 24 日。

应用无需账号，未接入广告或分析服务。开发者不接收应用的对局数据。附近对战通过你选择的局域网或蓝牙发现设备，并在两部手机间交换房间信息、加密的对局消息及重连凭据。系统的蓝牙配对记录由 Android 管理。

正常完赛后双方会看到对方的摆位用于核对。铅笔标记仅留在本机，离开棋桌或重新开局后清除。布局、对局和重连凭据留在进程内，退出棋桌或结束应用进程后清除。在支持附近设备权限的 Android 版本上，系统会请求授权后再扫描、连接或被对方发现。Android 11 及更早版本的系统可能要求位置权限以进行蓝牙发现。应用不读取地理位置。

Privacy policy

Applies to Android package com.amao.paperplanes. Effective November 24, 2026.

The app has no accounts, ads or analytics. The developer does not receive match data. On the local Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth selected by you, devices use room details and trade encrypted match messages and a reconnection credential. Android manages its own Bluetooth pairing records.

Both players see the opposing layout after a completed game. Pencil marks stay on your device and are removed when you leave the table or start a new round. Match data and reconnection credentials live in memory until you leave the table or the app process ends. Android versions with the Nearby devices permission request it for Bluetooth discovery, connection, and discoverability. Android 11 and earlier may request location permission for Bluetooth discovery. The app does not read your geographic location.
