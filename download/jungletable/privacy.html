Privacy and support

Jungle Table (com.amao.jungletable) offers offline practice, shared-device play, and nearby two-device games. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer game server. The app is distributed by amao (lihywh). The developer does not receive your moves, device contacts, location, or camera images from the app.

When you choose nearby play, the app discovers and connects directly to other devices on your local Wi-Fi or over Bluetooth. Room information, the system Bluetooth device name where available, the moves shared by the two players, and a temporary reconnection credential are used for that game. Devices have to confirm a matching code. Game traffic uses the connection between the two devices. Android’s network permission enables local connections. For Bluetooth hosting, the app asks Android to make the device discoverable to nearby devices for a limited period. The app discards the room information and the temporary credential when the session ends.

On Android 11 and earlier, Android requests location permission for Bluetooth discovery. The app does not request your GPS coordinates. Newer systems can ask for nearby devices or local-network access. The optional QR scanner uses the camera to read the room address, without retaining camera images. Declining these permissions still lets you practice or play on one device.

Language, sound, motion, and display preferences are stored on the device. Clear the app data or uninstall to remove them. If you contact support by email, your message and email address are used to answer the request and kept only as long as needed for that conversation. Changes are posted on this page with the effective date.

For support or privacy questions, email lihywh@gmail.com.

隐私政策与支持

斗兽棋同桌提供离线练习、同机双人和附近双机。无需账号，应用无广告、分析或游戏服务器。应用由 amao（lihywh）发行。开发者不会从应用收到你的棋谱、联系人、位置或相机画面。

选择附近双人时，应用通过局域网或蓝牙直接发现和连接另一台设备。房间资料、适用时的系统蓝牙设备名、对局走法和临时重连凭据只用于此次连接。两台设备需核对相同数字才能开始。Android 的网络权限用于建立局域网连接。蓝牙房主会请求系统在有限时间内让另一台设备发现本机。会话结束后应用丢弃房间信息与临时凭据。Android 11 及较早系统使用定位权限来扫描蓝牙，应用不请求 GPS 坐标。较新系统可请求附近设备或本地网络权限。扫描房间码时才用相机读取地址，不保留画面。拒绝授权仍能玩本机模式。

语言、音效、动态与棋子显示设置保存在本机，清除应用数据或卸载可移除。联系支持时，你主动发送的邮箱与内容用于回复，仅在处理该对话所需期间保留。政策变更在本页更新生效日期。问题请来信 lihywh@gmail.com。
