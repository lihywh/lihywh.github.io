PaperFold Studio privacy policy

This policy covers the PaperFold Studio Android app, package com.amao.origami. The app runs locally on your device. It does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, payment services or a backend.

The app stores your lesson progress, completion marks and language choice on your device. The app does not send that information to its developer or share it with third parties. You can remove it by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

If you contact the developer by email, your address and the information you provide are used to respond to your request. This support interaction occurs outside the app.

Publisher and privacy contact: lihywh. Email lihywh@gmail.com.

The browser demo also saves progress and language locally in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts this website and serves the APK download.

折纸手帖隐私说明

应用离线运行，课程进度、完成标记和语言选择只保存在本机。应用不接收或传输这些信息。可以在 Android 设置里清除应用数据，或卸载应用。若主动通过邮件联系客服，开发者会使用你提供的联系方式和内容回复支持请求。
