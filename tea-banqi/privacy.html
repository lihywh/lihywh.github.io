Tea House Dark Chess Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026. This policy covers the Android app Tea House Dark Chess (茶館暗棋 / 茶馆暗棋), package com.amao.teabanqi.

What the app does

The app runs without an account. It does not send your information to servers operated by the app developer. It contains no advertising, analytics, or purchase SDKs. Computer practice and play on one device work offline.

Nearby games

When you choose nearby play, devices use a shared local Wi-Fi network, phone hotspot, or Bluetooth. A room can advertise its name and local address so devices can find it. Connected devices exchange the moves and public game state needed to play and reconnect. This traffic stays between the participating devices. An Android Bluetooth device name and system pairing information may be visible to nearby devices through Android’s discovery and pairing features. The host device keeps the unrevealed positions in memory during the game.

Android may ask for nearby device or local network access. On Android 11 and earlier, the operating system requires location permission to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. The app does not read or store GPS locations. You can decline these permissions and still play against the computer or use the same-device mode.

Sharing and invites

The home screen shares the official download link through Android. In Nearby play, you can share the installer through Android, show a Wi-Fi connection code or display a separate code for a local download. You choose who receives the link or file. The developer does not receive a sharing record.

Choosing a local download starts a temporary HTTP service on the device. Someone on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot with the download URL can retrieve the APK. An invitation from a compatible hosted room also offers a link to join that room. Android handles installation. The service stops when you stop sharing, the invitation activity is destroyed, the network changes, or the ten-minute invitation expires.

If your installation cannot supply a complete installer, you can prepare a verified APK from the official website. The app may cache the file for offline sharing. Clearing the app cache removes these copies.

Website and links

The app can open the official website, https://lihywh.github.io/tea-banqi/, in your browser. That site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading it, or downloading the app from it, sends standard request details (such as IP address) to GitHub under GitHub’s own privacy policy. The developer does not add tracking to the site.

Retention and control

The app holds an active game and reconnect credentials in memory. If you leave the game or Android ends the process, that state is discarded. System Bluetooth pairing is managed in Android Settings. The app does not use Android Backup for its application data.

Contact

For privacy questions, email lihywh@gmail.com. Email sent to support is used to respond to your request.

茶館暗棋隱私政策

Android 可能要求附近裝置或區域網路權限。Android 11 及以下版本掃描藍牙時，系統需要位置權限。本 App 不讀取或保存 GPS 位置。拒絕附近連線權限後，仍可使用人機練習或同機雙人。

離開棋桌或系統結束 App 後，記憶體中的對局與重連資訊會被清除。本 App 不備份應用程式資料。隱私問題請寄信至 lihywh@gmail.com，支援信件只用於回覆你的請求。
