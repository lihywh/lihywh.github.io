ReturnPocket Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26 · Applies to the Android app "ReturnPocket" (com.amao.returnpocket)

Your data stays on your device

ReturnPocket stores everything locally: your purchase records, dates, notes, amounts, receipt images and settings live in the app's private storage on your device. The app holds no internet permission and does not automatically connect to any server operated by the developer or anyone else, and does not transmit your records on its own.

No collection, no tracking

The developer does not collect, receive or share any of your data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no third-party trackers; there are no developer servers, no cloud sync and no logins.

Receipts and images

Receipt images you attach are copied into the app's private storage (resized for bounded storage use, orientation preserved). They never leave the device unless you export them yourself.

Backups you control

Backup is voluntary: you choose where to save an export file through the system document picker. If you pick a cloud storage app or service, the file is saved through that app under its own privacy policy — ReturnPocket itself never uploads anything. Exports are plain, unencrypted zip files containing your records and receipt images — anyone with access to the file can read it, so store it somewhere private. Restoring asks for explicit confirmation and replaces current data with the backup contents.

Notifications

Optional deadline reminders are local notifications, off by default. You can enable or disable them in the app; delivery always follows your system notification and battery settings, and the app does not guarantee that a reminder will arrive.

Honest limits

ReturnPocket records information you enter manually. It does not verify retailer decisions, does not read your bank or store accounts, and does not provide legal advice about return rights.

Deletion

Deleting individual records removes them and their receipt copies. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. Exported backup files are yours to manage and delete.
