APK 批量管理器隐私政策

适用于 Google Play 应用 Apk Manager 4.0，包名 aMao.apkManager。更新日期为 2026 年 11 月 20 日。English version

扫描、安装与备份

应用在设备上读取您选择的安装包、获准访问的目录、系统文件索引和可见的已安装应用，以显示图标和版本、检查签名、生成备份和调用系统安装器。文件路径、应用列表、安装包、备份、扫描快照和安装记录保存在本机。应用不把这些内容传给广告平台。导出或分享时，由您选择文件及目的地。

广告与隐私选项

在启用广告的 Play 版本中，结果页可显示原生广告，您也可主动观看激励视频。Google AdMob 为投放和衡量广告、分析性能及防范欺诈，可能收集并分享 IP 地址及由此推断的大致位置、启动和广告互动、诊断及性能信息、广告 ID、应用组 ID 和其他设备或账号标识符。SDK 使用 TLS 传输数据。广告曝光记录和临时权益的到期时间保存在本机。

应用使用 Google 用户消息平台处理适用地区的广告隐私选择。需要时可在设置的“隐私选项”中变更选择，也可在 Android 系统设置中管理广告 ID。Google 对其收到的数据适用其自身的隐私和保留规则。Google 如何使用合作伙伴应用的数据

反馈、保留与删除

应用无需账号。选择反馈会打开邮件草稿，其中可包含 Android API 版本、批次状态及错误说明。您可以编辑、删除或放弃草稿。发送后，开发者会收到您提供的邮箱地址、正文和主动附上的材料，用于处理反馈。请勿发送私钥、口令或无关文件。

本机数据可通过 Android 设置中的“清除存储”或卸载移除。在其他位置自行保存的导出文件需单独删除。若要查询或请求删除发给开发者的反馈，请使用原发件地址联系 lihywh@qq.com，并注明应用名称和邮件日期，以便查找。有关 Google 所处理数据的管理方式可参阅 Google 隐私政策。

Apk Manager privacy policy

This policy covers Apk Manager 4.0 for Google Play, package aMao.apkManager. Updated November 20, 2026.

Files and installed apps

The app reads the packages you select, authorized folders, the system file index and installed apps visible to it. It uses them on your device to display icons and versions, check signatures, create backups and request installation through Android. Files, paths, installed-app lists, backups, snapshots and batch history stay on your device. The app does not send them to the ad provider. You choose the destination when exporting or sharing.

Ads and privacy choices

The ad-supported Play build can display native ads on batch results and offer optional rewarded videos. For advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, Google AdMob may collect and share IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app launches and ad interactions, diagnostics and performance data, advertising IDs, app set IDs and other device or account identifiers. The SDK encrypts transmissions using TLS. The app keeps exposure limits and reward expiry locally.

The Google User Messaging Platform handles applicable advertising privacy choices. Where available, reopen Privacy options in Settings to change your choices. Android also provides advertising ID controls. Google's own privacy and retention rules apply to the data it receives. How Google uses data from partner apps.

Support, retention and deletion

No account is required. Feedback opens an editable email draft that may include your Android API level, batch status and diagnostic messages. If you send it, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include to respond to your request. Please omit keys, passwords and unrelated files.

Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove its local records. Delete files you exported separately. To inquire about or request deletion of feedback you sent, email lihywh@qq.com from the original address and identify the app and message date. See Google's privacy policy for controls over data processed by Google.
